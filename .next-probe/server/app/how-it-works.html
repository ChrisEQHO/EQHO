How EQHO Player works

Prepare the running order before training, choose your session controls and let EQHO handle the music while you coach.

eqho-player.com/app
  1. 1

    Create your playlists

    Upload your routine music and organise it into the playlists you need.

  2. 2

    Arrange the running order

    Place routines in the order you want to run them.

  3. 3

    Set the session controls

    Choose the gap, set repeats and turn on back-to-back playback when needed.

  4. 4

    Press play and coach

    EQHO follows the running order while showing the current and next routine.

  5. 5

    Push sessions to EQHO Cloud

    When you choose to push a playlist, its audio and session information are saved to your EQHO account.

  6. 6

    Prepare your other devices

    Log in on each supported device and load or download the pushed playlists you need.

  7. 7

    Check before training

    Downloaded playlists can be used offline. Check them before travelling.
